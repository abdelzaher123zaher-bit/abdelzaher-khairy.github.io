Measuring water content in food materials using ovens & moisture analyzers — a key quality parameter for flour, powder & dry products.

Using refractometers to measure dissolved sugar concentration in juices & beverages — critical for formula control and final product quality.
قياس اللزوجة
Viscosity Measurement
تحديد مقاومة السوائل الغذائية للتدفق باستخدام أجهزة الفسكوميتر — مؤشر جودة رئيسي للصلصات والكريمات والمشروبات السميكة.
Determining flow resistance of food liquids using viscometers — a key quality indicator for sauces, creams & thick beverages.

Gas-liquid chromatography for analyzing organic compounds, fatty acids & pesticides in food samples — high analytical precision.

Monitoring frying oil quality by measuring smoke point, free fatty acids & oxidation levels — consumer protection & food safety assurance.

Evaluating food products via all five senses: appearance, aroma, texture, taste & sound — the gold standard for consumer satisfaction.
